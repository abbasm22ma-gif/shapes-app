Toolbar Guide

  • Undo / Redo (Ctrl+Z/Y): Reverts or reapplies your last action.
  • Save PNG: Downloads your diagram as an image file.
  • Dark Mode: Toggles the canvas background between light and dark.
  • Delete: Removes the currently selected shape (also Backspace/Delete).
  • Zoom Slider: Scales the canvas view in or out.
  • Clear All: Wipes the canvas completely to start fresh.
  • Add Shapes (+): Click to add a new Concept Oval or Arrow to the centre.
  • Connect (🔗): Click the button, then click Oval A, then Oval B to draw an auto-attached arrow.
  • Label Box: Select a shape (especially arrows) and type here to add the verb describing the relationship.
  • Rotate Slider: Spins the selected arrow around its centre.
  • Scale Slider: Makes the selected shape bigger or smaller.
